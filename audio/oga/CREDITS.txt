BLIND-FRIENDLY VIBE DESIGN:
---------------------------
For blind players, real audio provides crucial spatial and atmospheric info:

1. MUSIC VIBE (Noir Jazz):
   - Each district has its own real track (Future Noir for main, Velvet Sax for Velvet Room, etc.)
   - Crossfade between locations so blind player knows they entered new area
   - Real saxophone and jazz = authentic detective feeling vs synthetic
   - TTS announces track description for context

2. FOOTSTEPS (Navigation):
   - Real footstep sounds alternate left/right (stereo) - blind player feels walking
   - Different surfaces: concrete (southside), wood (chapel/mansion), wet (harbor)
   - Walking pace 450ms interval - natural rhythm
   - Stops when riding Bonnie

3. DOORS & LOCKS (Spatial Cues):
   - Real door open/close when entering POI - tells blind player they arrived
   - Lock open sound for puzzles (jingle lock, safe)
   - Glass break for action scenes

4. FIGHT SFX (Combat Feel):
   - Real meaty punch sounds from DavidW pack - 5 variations random
   - Stereo pan random for realism (punch from left/right)
   - Heavy punch louder, different file
   - Grunt layered for exertion

5. AMBIENT LOOPS (City Life):
   - Highway loop for Midtown traffic - blind player hears city alive
   - Construction for Industrial Row
   - Water for Harbor
   - City ambient for Southside
   - Plays subtly under music

6. BEACONS (Navigation Aid):
   - Switch sound panned left/right to indicate direction to landmark
   - Volume decreases with distance
   - Sonar ping in 3D space

All real audio can be toggled in Settings -> Blind-Friendly Real Audio
Falls back to generative synthesis if disabled or files missing.
